## Synthetic Visual Assets

### Deepwater Port Night Still & Drone Story
- **File:** `frontend/public/media/source/port-night.png` and responsive WebP/AVIF variants in `frontend/public/media/`.
- **Origin:** Synthetic night render of an industrial deepwater bulk maritime port terminal.
- **Processing:** Resized and optimized into responsive multi-resolution WebP and AVIF formats, 3:4 mobile crops, and low-res blurred placeholders via Lanczos resampling and SVT-AV1 encoding. All overlays (telemetry cones, draft lines, load bars, and metric cards) are drawn programmatically in SVG and HTML.
